Communication, Web, Design, and Curiosity.

Hi, I'm Kaho.

I've worked across a few different fields in Taiwan and Japan — education, web and graphic design, and hospitality.

The work has changed, but one thing has stayed constant: paying attention to the person and situation in front of me, and thinking about how to make things clearer.

About Me

I graduated from the Faculty of Fine Arts at Kyoto City University of Arts, where I focused on printmaking and animation.

After graduating, I worked as a Japanese-language teacher in Tainan, Taiwan, for about three years — mostly teaching beginner classes, using Chinese to communicate with students, and also helping with SNS posts and school events.

Back in Japan, I worked in web and graphic design in Osaka — producing web pages, landing pages, banners, and flyers using HTML/CSS, Photoshop, and Illustrator.

I now work at a hostel in Kyoto. My main responsibilities are supporting international guests, building and updating the website, organizing information, and improving day-to-day operations. I also use Google Sheets, WordPress, Google Search Console, and Canva.

How I Work

  • Look first. Start by paying attention to the other person and the situation.
  • Make it clear. Communicate in a way that's easy to understand.
  • Try and adjust. Try it, then adjust if needed.
  • Learn as I go. Look things up and learn as I go, when I don't know something.
  • Play the long game. Value what pays off later — the long way around, steady groundwork — even when it isn't the immediate payoff.

Looking Forward

Going forward, I'd like to combine the communication, web, design, information organization, and process-improvement experience I've built up, and expand into work that supports people and businesses more directly.

In a new environment, I want to keep learning by looking things up as I go, and gradually grow what I can do.

While staying engaged with my work, I want to keep learning, stay connected to culture, and value relationships with people — building a way of working I can keep up for the long run.
