About

米山佳穂 / Kaho Yoneyama

After university, I worked in hospitality — hotels and cafés — in tourist areas of Hokkaido, Shimane, and Kagawa. I then spent about three years in Taiwan as a Japanese-language teacher, where I also ran an Instagram account teaching Japanese to learners in Chinese-speaking regions. Back in Japan, I worked in web and graphic design, and I currently handle international guest support, website development and updates, and guest information at a hostel in Kyoto. Going forward, I'd like to settle in Taiwan and build my career here for the long term.
